Questions

Good questions.

Do you really put Santa hats on street cats?

Only on a few tame community cats that already know us, for a few seconds, never held down. Most cats just get a tiny hat placed next to their bowl. The hats on Cat ID cards are digital.

Is $CATMAS an investment?

No. $CATMAS is a memecoin with no intrinsic value and no promise of returns. Crypto is volatile — only spend what you're fine losing. The cats get fed from creator fees, not from your purchase.

How do I know the money reaches the cats?

Every Monday we post the on-chain withdrawal, the exchange rate, the shop receipts and dated feeding videos. The US share goes through pump.fun's Charity Coins with Donate.gg, which is verifiable on-chain.

Is my Cat ID number really unique?

Yes. Each registered card takes the next number in the Catmas registry, so no two cats share one.

Do you also fix (sterilize) the cats?

Feeding without sterilizing grows the stray population, so every 1,000 meals unlocks a sterilization round with a local vet partner.
